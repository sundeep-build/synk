Synk turns listening into a hangout. Start a room, invite your friends, and everyone hears the same song or live radio station at the same second, whether they're in the next room or another city.

LISTEN TOGETHER, IN SYNC
• Create a room in seconds: public for anyone, or private with a join code
• Music mode for songs and music videos, radio mode for live stations
• Playback stays in sync for everyone, and the host plays, pauses and skips for the room
• Share an invite link and friends jump straight in
• If the host leaves, someone else takes over so the music keeps going
• Reopen the rooms you host any time, or end them for good

MORE THAN A PLAYLIST
• Live chat next to the music
• Floating emoji reactions
• Dedicate a song to someone in the room
• A shared queue everyone can add to, with vote-to-skip
• When the queue runs out, Synk keeps going with similar songs

HUDDLES: TALK OVER THE MUSIC
• Start a voice call inside any room, with up to 8 people
• Turn your camera on (up to 4 people on video), switch cameras, mute, or use the speaker
• The huddle keeps going when you switch to another app

DISCOVER
• Trending music videos for your country
• 50,000+ live radio stations from around the world, including Bollywood, Punjabi and Hindi stations
• Radio that matches your vibe, and stations near you
• Genre pages with videos and stations
• Search for songs, videos and stations

YOUR LIBRARY
• Like songs and stations
• Make playlists
• Pick up where you left off with recently played

EASY TO START
• Look around as a guest, no sign-up needed
• Sign in with Google when you want to keep your profile
• Choose a username and build an emoji avatar
• Radio keeps playing in the background, with lock-screen and notification controls
• Dark, light or automatic theme
• Delete your account any time from your profile
• Free, with no ads

GOOD TO KNOW
Songs and music videos play in the official YouTube player while Synk is on screen, and pause when you leave the app. Live radio comes from the community-run Radio Browser directory. Synk is an independent app and is not affiliated with YouTube.
